Key Rules Before You Plant (PDF Page 13)

Crucial commercial lessons from established culinary farms (The Chef's Garden, Les Jardiniers du Chef) before investing in land or seeds:

⚠️

Highest Price ≠ Most Profitable

Selling Oyster Leaf at $0.50 per leaf does not mean a restaurant buys thousands of leaves every week. Always evaluate actual order volume, harvest labor, waste sorting, and seasonality before allocating greenhouse space.

📦

Catalog Price vs. Farm Gate Margin

Prices in professional purveyor catalogs reflect packing materials, sorting labor, chilled courier freight, and distributor margins. Net farmer profit depends on direct chef relationships and minimal handling loss.

🤝

Consult Chefs Before Sowing

Interview executive chefs and pastry masters in your target radius. Ask what rare botanicals they struggle to obtain reliably. Growing crops pre-committed to local tasting menus guarantees 100% sales at premium prices.
