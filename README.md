# 🌿 Haute Botanique · High-Value Culinary Crop Portal for Specialty Farmers

> **The Living Palette of Michelin Gastronomy** — An open-access agronomic knowledge base and commercial feasibility portal designed to help boutique farmers, greenhouse managers, and smallholder agriculturalists cultivate high-margin rare culinary crops for fine-dining restaurants.

## 🌟 Overview & Mission

Most agricultural literature focuses on commodity crops (tomatoes, lettuce, standard brassicas) requiring massive acreage to achieve modest profitability. Meanwhile, three-star Michelin kitchens and luxury hotels pay premium prices (e.g., **$0.50+ per single leaf** of Oyster Leaf, **$160/kg** for Finger Lime, or **$400/kg** for genuine fresh Wasabi rhizomes).

**Haute Botanique** bridges this critical gap by providing boutique growers with:
1. **Dedicated Agronomic Cultivation Guides** for over 60+ verified luxury cultivars.
2. **Saline & Halophyte Dosing Protocols** to induce signature ocean minerality and cellular crispness.
3. **The 500m² Commercial Greenhouse Allocation Blueprint** (modeled to produce **$115,000+** in annual gross revenue).
4. **Pillar 3: The Interactive ROI & Packaging Cost Calculator** guaranteeing at least a **40% net profit margin**.
5. **Cold-Chain & Precision Harvest Protocols (2°C - 4°C)** compliant with Michelin purveyor standards.

## 🚀 Key Modules & Interactive Features

### 1. Dedicated Single Crop Manuals (`plant.html?id=...`)
Each crop has its own dedicated page complete with:
* **Quick Agronomic Matrix**: Optimal temperatures, soil pH, stratification duration, spacing, and irrigation.
* **5-Stage Step-by-Step Growing Protocol**:
  1. *Seed Preparation & Cold Stratification* (e.g. 4-week 3°C cold-moist treatment for *Mertensia maritima*).
  2. *Plug Sowing & Transplanting*.
  3. *Soil Chemistry & Organic Fertility* (calcium-rich substrates, avoiding excessive nitrogen).
  4. *Saline Irrigation Protocol* (1% sea salt solutions for halophytes).
  5. *Integrated Biological Pest Management*.
* **Precision Harvest & Cold Chain Guidelines**: Morning turgor picking, leaf-crown regeneration, packaging in nitrogen-cushioned clamshells.
* **Fine Dining Applications**: Why chefs pay top dollar, signature 3-star dish examples, and heat-sensitivity warnings.
* **One-Click Printable Field Sheet** (`window.print()`) for greenhouse staff.

### 2. The 500m² Commercial Greenhouse Blueprint
* Interactive poly-tunnel allocation based on empirical purveyor models:
  * **Oyster Leaf**: 70 m² (14%)
  * **Red & Green Shiso**: 70 m² (14%)
  * **Haute Edible Flowers**: 100 m² (20%)
  * **Specialty Herbs (Nepitella/Pipicha)**: 80 m² (16%)
  * **Agretti & Crystalline Ice Plant**: 80 m² (16%)
  * **Forced Sea Kale**: 50 m² (10%)
  * **Experimental R&D**: 50 m² (10%)
* Dynamic scale slider calculating projected sales, harvest biomass in kilograms, and number of restaurant client accounts.

### 3. Pillar 3: ROI & Packaging Cost Calculator
* Allows the farmer to input specific local costs:
  * Monthly electricity & heating ($/month)
  * Packaging cost per clamshell ($ for food-grade clamshell + unbleached damp parchment paper + label)
  * Leaves per clamshell (20 to 100 leaves)
  * Monthly harvest volume
  * Labor & sorting costs
* Calculates:
  * Break-even production cost per leaf
  * **Minimum Recommended Selling Price per leaf** guaranteeing $\ge 40\%$ net profit margin
  * Minimum wholesale pack price
  * Monthly & annual clean net profit projection
  * **Market Competitiveness Analysis** benchmarked against Californian and Parisian wholesale purveyors ($0.50 - $0.65/leaf).

### 4. Haute Cuisine Chef Pairing Matrix
* Explains the culinary synergy between rare botanicals and luxury ingredients:
  * **Raw Oysters & Belon**: Oyster Leaf, Crystalline Ice Plant, Finger Lime.
  * **A5 Wagyu Beef**: Fresh Shizuoka Wasabi, Finger Lime, Pipicha.
  * **Scallops & Langoustines**: Marsh Samphire, Crosnes, Shiso.
  * **Foie Gras & Game**: Oca Confit, Nepitella, Sea Kale.
  * **Haute Pastry**: Szechuan Buzz Button, Yacon Ground Apple.

## 🛠️ Local Development & Quickstart

The project is built with zero external dependencies (pure modern HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript), ensuring blazing-fast load times and complete offline durability.

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/sofianechebilk/haute-botanique.git
   cd haute-botanique
   ```

2. **Serve locally:**
   ```bash
   # Using Python 3
   python -m http.server 8085
   ```

3. **Open in your browser:**
   Navigate to [http://localhost:8085](http://localhost:8085).
